Benchling and Signals work in the other half of daily R&D: years of experiments already live inside the scientist’s workbench. Here the design question is concrete: the experiment is already open—how can AI help carry it forward without losing the trail?

再追问两步，露出了两处隐蔽的坑PUSHING FURTHER EXPOSED TWO HIDDEN TRAPS

图表画得丝毫不差
总结却悄悄少算一个The chart was exact
The summary was one short

紧接着我追问“IC50 小于 100 nM 的有多少个”。AI 画出的分布图里，三个区间清清楚楚标着 1、42、35（相加是 78），紧挨着的文字总结却笃定地写着 77。图对、字错——这是严肃研发软件里最危险的错法：因为前面 43 条全对、图表又工整，人的防备心已经放下了。直到我指出矛盾、要求回查原附件，AI 才改口为 78，承认数错了。Next I asked how many values fell below 100 nM. Its own chart plotted bins of 1, 42 and 35 (which add up to 78)—yet the summary sentence right beside it confidently stated 77. Right chart, wrong sentence: the hardest error to catch in R&D software, because 43 accurate hits have already lowered the scientist’s guard. Only after I flagged the mismatch and forced a source recheck did the AI correct it to 78.

随后的三分子对比里，AI 又把“>1000 nM”过度推断成了“实验最高只测到 1000 nM”。原始文件根本没写测试浓度上限。我当场要求它删去臆测，只保留有原始记录支撑的下限事实。In a follow-up comparison, the AI over-reached again—treating “>1000 nM” as proof that 1000 nM was the highest concentration tested. The source file gave no assay ceiling. I required it to strip that inference and retain only the reported lower bound.

项目初期，团队内部曾认真讨论过另起炉灶，做一个独立于现有系统之外的全新 AI Agent 工具。上台演示起来更轻巧，但我一直反对把科学家从原工作台里拽出去：药企客户真正的护城河，是长年累月沉淀在 Signals One 里的实验记录、结构式与权限体系。Early on, the team debated spinning up a brand-new standalone AI agent app. A clean-slate chat product is easier to demo, yet I pushed back from what customers valued most: years of structured assays, chemical drawings and governed records already living inside Signals One.

我坚持的方向是：让 Signals AI 贴着已有实验数据生长。科学家不用切去新软件重新上传背景，后续三个核心交互决策也全部围绕“不脱离现场、随时查回原记录”展开。I argued that Signals AI should grow directly around that existing data. Scientists shouldn’t have to re-upload files or lose audit trails in a separate tool—and the three interaction decisions below all follow from keeping AI anchored to the live record.

01 / Signals AI

让提问留在实验旁边Keep the question beside the experiment

科学家明明正打开着实验记录，没道理再向对话框口述一遍“我在看哪份表”。我采用分栏并排架构，让侧边对话直接读取当前实验、关联应用与附件上下文，视线左右一扫就能原地核对。When an experiment is already open on screen, a scientist should never have to re-describe it in a prompt. A split-pane layout binds the conversation directly to the active record, app and attachments so eyes can cross-check in place.

设计代价：让出右侧一栏画布宽度，换取零上下文丢失与同屏比对。Trade-off: sacrifice horizontal canvas width to preserve live context and side-by-side verification.
02 / Signals AI

先亮出筛选口径，再谈结论Expose the filter rule before asking for trust

自然语言极易吞掉逻辑细节：“小于”含不含等于？nM 和 μM 换算了吗？带“>”的下限值算不算过线？我要求回答把解析后的单位、比较符与剔除规则明文摊在结果上方，把黑盒问答变成可审阅的查询。Natural language quietly swallows boolean precision: is it < or ≤, were units normalized, what happened to “>” bounds? Surfacing the parsed unit, operator and exclusion rule right above the shortlist turns a black-box answer into an inspectable query.

设计代价：回答顶部多占一行规则区，消灭因口径误读导致的整批错筛。Trade-off: spend one extra line of vertical space to eliminate silent criteria misinterpretation.
