## First interaction storyboard

1. Rest: room already composed, no entrance animation; sound off.
2. Focus (120 ms): hovered/focused turntable gets a small label and thin light edge; touch requires no hover.
3. Open (450 ms): vinyl lifts slightly, camera nudges toward console, DOM music panel opens. Reduced motion opens panel immediately.
4. Play: explicit user press starts supported player. Third-party player readiness is not proof of playback. External link remains available; no fake progress/waveform.
5. Enter live: a separate action opens the real CALL ME YAO MV, with a short skippable transition; no implicit fullscreen.
6. Return (300 ms): dispose player, restore overview and focus. Mode switch immediately navigates Classic, regardless of loading/animation.
