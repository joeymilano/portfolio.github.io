Overview

A content workflow
built for small teams

One product update often meant rewriting the same story for every channel. I designed the workspace around that task: add a source, choose channels, then edit and check the drafts.

My contribution

I shaped the core tasks, reorganized the workspace, and designed the bilingual, light and dark interface system. With the team, I carried confirmation, cancellation, and recovery states into the product.

Design decisions

Keep context visible

01 / Context design

Make brand context visible and editable

Identity, positioning, and voice live together. People can see and edit the context behind AI-generated content.

Saved voice principle
Clear, credible, without overpromising
Joey Zhao · Person + brand

The design decision

Separate recurring context from the source for each task. Stable brand information and changing task inputs each have a clear home.
