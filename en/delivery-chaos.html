01 / 05

One more push

The first design gave you three big pounces per round. Now you can swipe whenever you like, with three super pounces in reserve. At the edge of the table, hold E to inch the cup forward. The paw has to touch it, and the fall goes on the bill.

You can also sneak up and swat the owner from behind. The reward only comes if you lose him, so pick your way out before you strike.

02 / 05

Sit. Look innocent.

A thin line across the rug shows where the camera can see. A quick pass gives you some leeway. Stay in view while making a mess and you lose time.

The owner comes after you when he notices trouble. Break his line of sight, sit and play innocent, or get close enough to knock the camera down or unplug it.

03 / 05

The living room has two levels

You can take the long way around the coffee table or jump up the steps to the catwalk. The orange cat has another reason to climb. A drop from height sends nearby objects flying.

04 / 05

How much trouble can two cats make?

Smash a hiding place to find a new cat. On their own, Ah Huai rebounds after a hit and Wumei meows on command. Fuse them into Noise Bomb and a collision during zoomies fires a shockwave that can send untouched objects flying.

05 / 05

Leave the bill for the owner

Every item has a price and appears on the bill once. The receipt adds up the cups, lamps and trouble you got away with. The cat gives one last meow and leaves the bill for the owner.

My part

I led gameplay direction, room layout, character presentation and UI decisions, and drove iteration on the Unreal prototype. The project also used contributor-supplied audio, licensed models and AI-assisted code and asset production. The public repository contains the shareable source.
