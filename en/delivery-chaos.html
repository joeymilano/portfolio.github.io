01 / 06

One last nudge

Tap J to swat or hold E to inch a cup forward. A little nudge leaves the bill untouched. Once the cup tumbles over the edge, you hear it break and see coins fly into the bill. Each object only counts once.

The early version gave you three big pounces per round, which made every strike feel expensive. Now you can swipe or pounce whenever you like. Hold J and the cat wiggles its rear to charge one of three super pounces. Or hit Shift and watch Ah Huai ricochet off a prop.

02 / 06

Quick, look innocent.

The pet camera draws its field of view on the rug. Walking through is fine. Get filmed causing trouble for about a second and the timed challenge loses two seconds. Sit still to earn a good-cat bonus, or sneak up and swat the camera down or unplug it. Taking it offline adds ¥399 to the bill.

Slip behind the owner and swat him before he turns. Get caught and you’re sent back to the cat bed, with 15 seconds off the clock in a timed round. Lose the chase and stay out of sight for two seconds to collect the ¥500 prank bonus.

03 / 06

The cat gets to the Halloween party first

The pumpkin lanterns, cake and candy are set out before the party starts. Knock over the bowl and 24 pieces of candy scatter across the floor while light objects start to bounce. The tallest pumpkin lantern is hiding a cat.

Go around the couch to break line of sight, or hop up eight steps to the catwalk. Jutuan has a second reason to climb. Dropping from up there sends nearby objects flying.

04 / 06

Ah Huai + Dafu = bad news for the living room

Break a hiding spot, then walk up to the cat you’ve found to bring it back to the lab. Fusion combines two instincts. Ah Huai usually bounces off whatever he hits. Fuse him with Wumei to make Noise Bomb and the impact also fires a fan-shaped shockwave.

05 / 06

A chase needs a beat

I borrowed the rhythm of a Tom and Jerry chase. The CanCan gives everyday mischief a quick beat. When the owner gives chase or a timed round reaches its last 20 seconds, the pursuit cue takes over. Escape early and the cue still gets to finish its run.

A falling cup gets a sharp crack. Coins hit the bill with a cash-register chime. At the end I give the cat one last meow, then let the mess speak for itself.

06 / 06

Leave the bill for the owner

At the end of a timed round, the receipt lists damaged objects and prank bonuses. The total goes on the local leaderboard. Free Play gives you time to learn the stairs and find the hiding spots. Any cats you find still come home with you.

My part

I led gameplay direction, room layout, character presentation and UI decisions, and drove iteration on the Unreal prototype. The project also used contributor-supplied audio, licensed models and AI-assisted code and asset production. The public repository contains the shareable source.
