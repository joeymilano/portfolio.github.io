AI for Drug DiscoveryAI 科研制药

The AI got 43 molecules right—then miscounted by one.AI 筛对了 43 个分子，却在总结里数错一个。

In a real public-assay screening review, the chart was exact and all 43 hits checked out—yet one summary sentence miscounted by one. Harmless in casual chat; costly in drug discovery. See how I designed scientific AI as an inspectable workbench rather than blind chat: keeping questions beside the live experiment, exposing the exact filter rule, and wiring every claim back to its source record.在一次真实的公开药物筛选复核里，图表画对了，43 条候选也全对，偏偏文字总结少算了 1 个。在闲聊软件里这只是小瑕疵，在药物研发里却可能带偏下一轮实验。这个案例讲我如何把科研 AI 从“给答案的对话框”设计成“可验算的工作台”：提问留在实验旁、筛选口径摆在明面、每条结论都能一键点回原始记录。
02 / SIGNALS NOTEBOOK View case study查看案例 →

Material Lineage Map材料谱系图

One material ID in hand. Where did it come from?手里只剩一个编号，怎么追回整条来路？

When an experiment behaves unexpectedly, a scientist often holds only one material ID: what was it derived from, which specific batch was used, and which assays touched it? Reconstructing that chain across scattered records used to take two hours. I connected materials, batches and experiments in an expandable lineage map paired with a synchronized verification table—cutting tracing tasks to 40 minutes.实验一旦出现异常，科学家手里往往只剩一个材料编号：它由什么衍生而来、用了哪一锅批次、进过哪次实验？过去在十几份记录间来回翻找要花两小时。我把材料、批次与实验串成可逐级展开的谱系图，并刻意保留了下方同步联动的核验表格——图看分支去向，表做逐行核对，将追溯耗时压到 40 分钟。
