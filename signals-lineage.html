When an experiment turns unexpected, a scientist often holds only one material ID and three urgent questions: what was it derived from, which exact batch was used, and which experiments touched it? Piecing that chain together across scattered records used to take two hours. I connected materials, batches and experiments in an expandable map paired with a live audit table.实验一旦出现意外结果，科学家手里往往只剩一个材料编号，却急需查清三件事：它由什么衍生而来、用了哪一锅具体批次、进过哪几次实验？过去在十几份记录间来回跳转、靠脑子拼关系，一查就是两小时。我把分散的材料、批次与实验串成可逐层展开的谱系图，并配上一张同步联动的核验表。

01 / THE INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE FLAW01 / 旧流程的断点

Experiments move forward.
Investigations run in reverse.做实验是顺着记，
查问题却要倒着追

During bench work, recording flows forward: open an experiment table and register a derived child material directly from it. Months later, when debugging an unexpected result, the direction flips completely: a scientist holds only a material ID and must work backward to recover its parents, lots and prior assays.平时做实验记录时，动作是顺向的：打开实验材料表，直接沿用信息登记子代材料。可一旦几个月后复盘异常结果，方向彻底倒了过来：科学家手里只有一个材料编号，却要倒推它的亲代来源、分装批次和历史实验。顺着建档的目录，成了倒着破案的迷宫。

THE ENTRY-POINT SHIFT关键入口重构

手里只有材料编号，
破案就该从材料开始。When all you hold is a material ID,
the investigation must start there.

我没有在系统外另造一个孤立的“图谱工具”，而是沿用科学家最熟悉的材料智能文件夹作为统一入口，在材料详情页里把“谱系（Lineage）”做成与属性、批次并列的原生页签——查清身份下一秒，就能原地展开上下游关系。Rather than inventing a standalone graph tool, I anchored entry in the familiar Materials Smart Folder and placed Lineage as a native peer tab beside Properties and Lots inside Material Details—turning identity lookup directly into relationship tracing.

最棘手的业务建模题THE DOMAIN MODELING CHALLENGE

材料同名，批次不同
实验结局可能截然相反Same material name,
different batches, different fates

同一种材料会分次制备出多个批次，每一批又会流向不同的下游实验。如果图谱只按“材料名称”连线，两轮一成一败的实验在图上看起来会一模一样，根本揪不出问题出在哪一管。One material produces multiple batches, each feeding different downstream experiments. Connect material names alone, and a failed run looks identical to a successful one—hiding which exact lot caused the divergence.

01

先看黑色节点：“它是什么”Black nodes: what the material is

草图里的黑色节点代表材料本身。比如这株病毒载体由三种上游质粒共同衍生，光在材料层就已经是多源汇合，绝非单线家谱。Black nodes mark material identities. Here a viral vector merges three upstream plasmids—already a multi-parent network rather than a single chain.

02

再追黄色节点：“用的哪一批”Yellow nodes: which lot went where

黄色节点代表具体批次。同一材料的两个批次分别流向不同细胞系，往下又继续分批。只追到黑色节点，离锁定那次异常实验还差着十万八千里。Yellow nodes track specific batches. Two lots split into separate cell lines, then branch into further sub-lots. Stopping at material names misses the real trail.

拆成两张图会逼人在脑子里手动拼图，全量摊开又会乱成毛线团。我的方案是用清晰的节点色彩区分当前对象、材料与批次，支持按需逐支展开，并在图谱下方同步保留批次与关联表格，让寻路与对账各司其职。Splitting into two maps forces mental stitching; expanding everything at once creates a hairball. I unified assets and batches with distinct visual encoding and on-demand branch expansion, backed by synchronized detail tables below.

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS & TRADE-OFFS03 / 关键设计取舍

Why I refused to let the map
replace the table图谱和表格，
为什么我坚持一个都不删

01

不造新入口，长在老习惯里Extend familiar navigation instead of siloing

材料智能文件夹管全局查找，详情页管单份档案。把谱系做成详情页里的原生页签，科学家零学习成本就能从“查身份”丝滑切入“追关系”。Materials Smart Folder handles discovery; Material Details holds the entity. Embedding Lineage as a native tab lets scientists pivot from identity check to tracing with zero relearning.

02

图展开到哪，表就跟到哪Lockstep sync between branches and rows

在 Miro 交互方案中，节点按需逐层展开，下方祖先与后代表格实时联动；悬停速览核心属性，菜单一键跳详情，碰到跨组无权限记录则明确给出受限提示——补齐这些异常状态，探索才不会断在半路。Branches expand on demand while ancestor and descendant tables update in lockstep; hover previews key fields, node menus open records, and restricted entries show explicit permission notices so investigations never hit silent dead ends.

03

图看拓扑去向，表做逐行对账Pay the dual-view cost for two distinct cognitive jobs

连线图擅长两秒看懂多源汇合与分叉，却不适合比对十几个字段和导出；表格正好反过来。同时维护双视图增加了前端工作量，但守住了“寻路 + 核验”的完整任务。而需要后端服务支撑的 PDF 与 API 导出，则与团队协商拆入二期，确保核心交互按期上线。Graphs reveal forks and multi-parent origins at a glance; tables handle dense field comparison and CSV export. Maintaining both views cost extra engineering effort, while server-rendered PDF and API exports were scoped into a follow-up phase to ship core tracing cleanly.

深挖一处微观交互决策ZOOMING IN ON ONE MICRO-DECISION

几十个专业字段，

从头翻到尾？Dozens of domain fields—

一条谱系链往往横跨多类生物材料：细胞系关心传代次数与生物安全等级，蛋白复合物关心重链轻链与总分子量，再加上通用审计字段，可选列轻松超过三十项。全塞进表格会撑爆横向滚动条，全摊成一列清单又让人翻到眼花。One lineage chain spans distinct biological libraries: Cell Lines track passage numbers and biosafety levels, Complexes track heavy/light chains and molecular weight, on top of common audit fields—easily 35+ columns. Showing all crushes the table; a flat checklist means endless scrolling.

我拆解出多套配置交互并在 Miro 中与产品经理 Adrian 逐一推演。最终我力推方案三：「先按材料类型收窄范围，再批量勾选移入」，并在迭代中补齐批量全选与字段排序，同时锁死必填的「材料编号」防止误删主键。After mapping multiple interaction options and debating edge cases with PM Adrian Papas in Miro, I championed Option 3: scope by material library first, then transfer fields individually or in bulk—locking mandatory Material ID while supporting column reordering.

在约五个月周期里，我与美国产品经理 Adrian Papas 及 10 位研发工程师并肩推进，独立负责材料追溯的信息架构重构、线框与高保真原型、科学家走查测试及最终界面落地。回头看，整个项目里最经得起时间检验的决定，是我顶住“只做炫酷关系图”的诱惑，把下方那张朴素的联动表格留了下来。图谱好看，两秒钟能看清分支走向；表格诚实，能一行行对准批号、筛选字段、导出成表。做科研工具，不能只讨好第一眼的惊艳，得陪科学家把证据查到底。Over roughly five months alongside US PM Adrian Papas and ten engineers, I owned the end-to-end IA shift, wireframes, interactive prototypes, scientist walkthroughs and shipped UI. Looking back, the decision that aged best was resisting the urge to ship “just a sleek node graph” and fighting to keep the plain, synchronized table right underneath it. Graphs look impressive in a pitch and reveal topology in seconds; tables are where scientists verify lot numbers, filter 30+ attributes and export audit proof. Good R&D design serves the whole investigation, not just the first glance.
